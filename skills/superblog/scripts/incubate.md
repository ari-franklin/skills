# Incubate

Purpose: determine whether an idea is worth turning into a blog post.

## Input

A rough idea, claim, note, quote, or workplace story.

## Output

- Core thesis
- Reader tension
- Underlying structural shift
- Novelty score: 1-5 with rationale
- Strength score: 1-5 with rationale
- Competing interpretations
- Missing evidence or story material
- Recommended direction
- Potential titles

## Decision Rule

Recommend drafting only when the idea has a specific claim, a real tension, and a reader who would behave or think differently after reading it.
