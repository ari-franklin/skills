# Outline

Purpose: turn an approved idea into a blog post structure before drafting.

## Output

- Working title
- One-sentence thesis
- Intended reader
- Opening scene or claim
- Section-by-section outline
- Key examples or stories to include
- Counterargument or competing interpretation
- Practical takeaway
- Ending move

## Quality Bar

Every section should have a job. If a section only repeats the thesis, merge it or cut it.
