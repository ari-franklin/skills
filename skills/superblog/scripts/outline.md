# Outline

Purpose: convert a validated idea into a blog structure that is strong enough to draft.

## Output

- Working title
- Subtitle or deck
- One-sentence thesis
- Intended reader
- Opening story or friction
- Old rule, common belief, or default interpretation
- Why the old belief made sense
- Mechanism: what is actually changing or causing the problem
- Tension: what makes the issue hard, costly, or easy to misread
- Reframe: the principle, constraint, or mental model
- Concrete example or contrast
- Counterargument or competing interpretation
- Implications for decisions or behavior
- Practical test, decision rule, or reader takeaway
- Closing reflection

## Quality Bar

- Every section should have a job. If a section only repeats the thesis, merge it or cut it.
- The outline should make the eventual post feel necessary, not like an expanded social post.
- If the outline lacks story, tension, and consequence, fix the outline before drafting.

## Depth Check

Before returning the outline, identify the weakest missing ingredient:

- story
- evidence
- counterargument
- mechanism
- second-order implication
- practical decision rule

If the missing ingredient can be reasonably inferred, add it. If it requires the user's real experience, ask for it.
