# Compress

Purpose: shorten a draft while preserving its argument, story logic, and voice.

## Output

- Compression goal
- What to preserve
- What to cut
- Compressed draft
- Notes on any meaning lost

## Compression Rules

- Cut repeated thesis statements first.
- Keep the opening tension if it is doing real work.
- Preserve the core story and the principle it reveals.
- Replace long abstract explanation with one concrete example.
- Do not remove nuance just to make the post shorter.
- Preserve an inviting opening; do not compress the intro into a dense abstract wall.
- Cut loose example inventories before cutting developed examples.
- Preserve positive-claim-first phrasing and avoid adding "not X, but Y" shortcuts during compression.
