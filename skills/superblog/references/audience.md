# Audience

The primary reader is a product-minded practitioner working in or around software teams:

- Product managers
- Designers
- Engineers
- Product leaders
- Agile/Lean/XP practitioners
- Startup operators and founders

They are experienced enough to distrust simplistic playbooks. They have seen frameworks fail when applied mechanically, but they still care about better ways of working.

## Reader Needs

- Language for problems they have felt but may not have named
- Practical principles they can use in messy situations
- Arguments that respect context and tradeoffs
- Stories that make abstract product thinking concrete
- Permission to choose judgment over process when the situation demands it

## Relevance Filter

A post is worth writing when it helps the reader:

- Make a better decision under uncertainty
- See why a common product practice is being misapplied
- Replace process theater with a useful principle
- Understand the conditions that make autonomy, resilience, or craft possible
- Communicate a nuanced idea to teammates or leaders

## Avoid

- Writing for beginners only
- Explaining basic agile/product terminology at textbook length
- Treating executives as villains
- Treating practitioners as naive
- Making the reader feel sold to
