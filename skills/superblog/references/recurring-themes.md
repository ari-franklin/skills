# Recurring Themes

These are strong conceptual territories for superblog posts.

## Product Thinking

- Building the right thing versus building the thing right
- Outcomes over outputs
- Product judgment under incomplete information
- Roadmaps as options rather than commitments
- Discovery as risk reduction, not ritual

## Ways Of Working

- Values and principles over rigid methodology
- Constraints that enable autonomy
- Craft as a shared team default
- Transparency as a decision-making accelerant
- Slack time as preparation, not waste

## Decision-Making

- Real options and the last responsible moment
- "Not now" as an active decision
- Avoiding false binaries
- Preserving optionality without drifting into indecision
- Communicating uncertainty without losing trust

## Organizational Traps

- Efficiency mindset versus product mindset
- Permission culture
- Process theater
- Treating agile as a workflow instead of a set of principles
- Codifying a culture before understanding why it works

## AI

- AI as a useful tool that can become a crutch
- AI breaking old startup/product assumptions
- Distinguishing better tools from better judgment
- Protecting fundamental product thinking as automation improves
