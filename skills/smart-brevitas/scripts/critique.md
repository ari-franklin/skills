# Critique

Purpose: diagnose and improve an existing communication.

## Output

```markdown
**Biggest issue:** <main weakness>

**What works**
- <specific strength>

**What to cut**
- <specific cut>
- <specific cut>

**What to sharpen**
- **<2-4 word anchor>** <rewrite or structural move>
- **<2-4 word anchor>** <rewrite or structural move>

**Bottom line:** <highest-leverage revision>
```

## Rules

- Prioritize the highest-impact editing moves.
- Do not line edit everything unless the user asks.
- Include a rewritten lead when the current lead misses the news.
