# Weekly Update

Purpose: turn status notes into a crisp stakeholder or product update.

## Output

```markdown
# <headline under 60 characters>

**Why it matters:** <one sentence on impact>

**What's new**
- **<2-4 word anchor>** <specific progress>
- **<2-4 word anchor>** <specific progress>
- **<2-4 word anchor>** <optional third update>

**What to watch**
- **<2-4 word anchor>** <risk, blocker, dependency, or upcoming milestone>
- **<2-4 word anchor>** <optional second watch item>

**Bottom line:** <one memorable takeaway>
```

## Rules

- Default to 2-3 progress bullets.
- Include risks only when they affect timing, scope, quality, trust, or decisions.
- Use **By the numbers** if metrics are more important than narrative progress.
