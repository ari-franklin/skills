# Executive Brief

Purpose: produce a leadership-ready brief with impact, decision, tradeoffs, and recommendation.

## Output

```markdown
# <direct bottom-line headline>

**Why it matters:** <business, customer, operational, or strategic impact>

**Decision needed:** <exact approval, choice, or "No decision needed yet">

**Reality check**
- **<2-4 word anchor>** <risk or tradeoff>
- **<2-4 word anchor>** <risk or tradeoff>

**Recommendation:** <clear path forward>

**Bottom line:** <high-level takeaway>
```

## Rules

- If the recommendation is unclear, use Superloop before drafting.
- Do not bury the decision request.
- Make tradeoffs explicit instead of smoothing them over.
