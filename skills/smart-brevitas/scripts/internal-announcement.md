# Internal Announcement

Purpose: explain an internal change clearly and warmly.

## Output

```markdown
# <action-oriented warm headline>

**Why it matters:** <how this affects the team>

**What's new**
- **<2-4 word anchor>** <change>
- **<2-4 word anchor>** <change>

**By the numbers**
- **<2-4 word anchor>** <date, deadline, size, or timing detail>

**What to watch**
- **<2-4 word anchor>** <action required, next milestone, or owner-dependent item>

**Bottom line:** <clear and human closing thought>
```

## Rules

- Omit **By the numbers** if there are no dates, deadlines, or metrics.
- Be direct about what is changing.
- Do not over-reassure. Clarity is kinder than vagueness.
