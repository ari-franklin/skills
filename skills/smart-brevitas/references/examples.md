# Examples

## Dense Source

```text
We had a good discussion about the checkout issues this week. The team reviewed the logs and found that a lot of the failures seem to be connected to timeout handling in the payment step. There is still some work to do, but we think the fix is promising. Legal also needs to review the pilot language before we communicate anything more broadly.
```

## Smart Brevitas Rewrite

```markdown
# Checkout timeout fix is ready for pilot

**Why it matters:** Payment failures are still the biggest risk to expanding the checkout pilot.

**What's new**
- **Timeout handling** is now the likely source of the payment-step failures.
- **A candidate fix** is ready for pilot testing after this week's log review.

**What to watch**
- **Legal review** is the gating item before broader pilot communication.

**Bottom line:** The technical fix is close, but legal approval now controls the pilot timeline.
```

```markdown
**What's new**

- **Payment logs** point to timeout handling as the likely checkout failure.
```

## Executive Brief Example

```markdown
# Approve checkout pilot expansion next week

**Why it matters:** Expanding the pilot gives us enough traffic to confirm whether the timeout fix materially reduces payment failures.

**Decision needed:** Approve expansion to 10% of eligible traffic after legal signs off on customer-facing language.

**Reality check**
- **Legal review** could delay the launch by 2-3 days.
- **Payment failures** may persist if the timeout fix only addresses one failure path.

**Recommendation:** Approve the expansion, but hold launch until legal signs off and support has the escalation path.

**Bottom line:** We should expand the pilot, but only with legal approval and support coverage in place.
```
