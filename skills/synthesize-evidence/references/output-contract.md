# Output Contract

Begin with the cadence, time window, and sources used. State any missing baseline or source limitation before the material items.

```markdown
# Evidence synthesis — <daily or weekly>, <time window>

Sources used: <attributable source list>
Prior synthesis or accepted items: <source, date, or unavailable>
Limitations: <only material gaps>

## Material items

### <Title>
- **Classification:** <one evidence-model classification>
- **What changed:** <direct, concise account>
- **Why it matters:** <source-backed consequence or clearly labeled interpretation>
- **Evidence:** <source identifiers and concise supporting detail; exact excerpts are verbatim and attributed>
- **Confidence:** <high | medium | low> — <reason>
- **What would change the view:** <specific corroborating, conflicting, or missing evidence>
- **Status:** <accepted | proposed>

## Strongest pattern
<pattern, its evidence, and confidence boundary>

## Important contradiction or weak signal
<conflict or minority signal and why it remains visible>

## Decisions made or needed
<explicit source-backed decisions and/or decisions that need an owner>

## Open questions
<material unresolved issues>

## Suggested next review
<who should review what, when, and the evidence needed next>
```

For `daily`, use proposed status by default and make the next review an approval or reclassification step for Ari. For `weekly`, show how the strongest pattern differs from the prior weekly synthesis: persistent, strengthening, weakening, new, or resolved.

Do not add a section merely to fill the template. Say "None identified in the provided sources" where an empty required summary is important context.
