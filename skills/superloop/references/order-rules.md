# Order Rules

## Purpose

Determine the sequence of reasoning modes.

The goal is not to execute every mode.

The goal is to close reasoning gaps in the correct order.

---

## Rule 0

If the reasoning gap is unclear:

Explain first.

---

## Rule 1

Do not prioritize what is not understood.

Understanding before decisions.

Example:

Bad:

Prioritize → Explain

Good:

Explain → Prioritize

---

## Rule 2

Do not decompose what is undefined.

Understanding before structure.

Example:

Bad:

Decompose → Explain

Good:

Explain → Decompose

---

## Rule 3

Do not validate unstated claims.

Make the claim explicit first.

Example:

Bad:

Validate

Good:

Explain → Validate

---

## Rule 4

Decision before structure.

When selecting among options:

Explain → Prioritize → Decompose

Reason:

There is little value in decomposing work that may not be selected.

---

## Rule 5

Structure before prioritization.

When the object itself is unclear:

Explain → Decompose → Prioritize

Reason:

You cannot rank what you do not understand.

---

## Rule 6

Validation before commitment.

The larger the commitment, the stronger the validation requirement.

Example:

Explain → Prioritize → Validate

before

major investment or execution.
